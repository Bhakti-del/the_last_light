# THE LAST LIGHT — TGC GAME JAM

**Genre:** Horror / Puzzle / Escape  
**Engine:** Godot  
**Themes:** Comic • Light • Twist  
**Playtime:** 10–15 Minutes  

---

## 1. Game Concept

*The Last Light* is a short first-person horror escape game set inside an abandoned house. The player wakes with no memory of how they arrived. Their only tool is a flashlight with limited battery. They must explore, find items, solve puzzles, and escape while avoiding a mysterious creature.

**Theme integration:** Light drives exploration and survival; Comic delivers clues and memories; Twist changes the player's understanding of the entire game.

---

## 2. Core Gameplay

**Explore → Use Light → Discover Clue → Find Item → Avoid Creature → Solve Puzzle → Progress**

- **Light ON:** reveals hidden objects and clues, enables navigation and some puzzles, but lets the creature detect the player.
- **Light OFF:** makes the player harder to detect, but hides important clues and makes exploration difficult.

> Risk / reward: *"Do I turn on the light to see, knowing that something might see me?"*

---

## 3. The Three Themes

### 💡 Light
The flashlight controls visibility, puzzles, story discovery, and enemy detection.

### 📖 Comic
Comic-style panels contain dialogue, drawings, hints, memories, and effects such as "BANG!" and "RUN!". Some appear only under the flashlight.

### 🔀 Twist
Records reveal **ATTEMPT #01, #17, #32** and **#46**. The player realizes the journey has happened before and their memories are reset each time.

> *Example: A blank wall becomes a comic panel when illuminated: "DON'T TRUST THE LIGHT." The panel disappears when the player moves away.*

---

## 4. Story

The player wakes in a dark bedroom. A message reads **"DON'T LET THE LIGHT DIE."** They find the flashlight and explore. Hidden comic panels reveal strange warnings while the creature reacts to the flashlight.

Near the end, evidence of previous attempts appears: **ATTEMPT #01 • #17 • #32 • #46**. The player discovers that the creature is connected to those previous attempts and that they have been repeating the same journey.

> **Final comic panel: "YOU HAVE BEEN HERE BEFORE."**

---

## 5. Level Structure

| Room | Description |
|---|---|
| **Bedroom** | Wake up; flashlight; movement and interaction. |
| **Hallway** | Comic clues; light tutorial; first creature encounter. |
| **Storage Room** | Key items and light-based puzzle. |
| **Living Room** | More panels and previous-attempt evidence. |
| **Basement** | Darker, dangerous encounters, major reveal. |
| **Exit Area** | Apparent escape followed by final clue. |
| **Final Room** | Comic sequence and repeating-cycle reveal. |

## 7. Scope

- First-person movement and interaction
- Flashlight + limited battery system
- One main creature / enemy
- Simple inventory, keys, and interactables
- Light-based puzzles
- Comic-style story panels
- Small interconnected house
- Hiding and chase sequences
- Environmental storytelling
- Sound effects and horror atmosphere
- Final story twist
- Browser-playable HTML5 / Web build

> The scope is intentionally small so a complete 10–15 minute experience can be finished within the jam.

## 9. Theme Dependency

- **LIGHT** → controls exploration and survival.
- **COMIC** → communicates clues and story.
- **TWIST** → changes the meaning of the clues and story.

> Removing any one of the three themes would significantly change the gameplay experience.

---

## Final Pitch

*The Last Light* is a short horror escape game where the flashlight is both the player's greatest advantage and greatest danger. Through comic-style clues, the player slowly uncovers a disturbing truth: this is not their first escape attempt.

The game combines **Comic**, **Light**, and **Twist** into one simple, interactive horror experience designed to be understood quickly and remembered after a 10–15 minute playthrough.
